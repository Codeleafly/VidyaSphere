Student Guide

How students join, learn, share notes, and message safely

1. Direct Registration & 1-Click Approval

Register with your Class (1–12), Division, Roll Number, Username, and Password. Your account stays on a real-time Waiting Screen until your Class Teacher or School Admin approves with 1 click — zero activation codes required!

2. Lessons, 5-Min Quizzes & Homework

Watch teacher video lessons on tap (data-saver mode never auto-loads video streams), attempt auto-graded 5-minute MCQ quizzes with instant answer-key explanations, and track daily homework with your local Done checklist.

3. Academic Notes (15MB Gzip & Drive)

Upload up to 4 direct PDFs/images per day (up to 15MB, gzip-compressed for 30 days) or share unlimited permanent Google Drive links valid for the entire academic year. Look for the Teacher Verified badge!

4. Exams, Q&A & School Polls

Check live countdowns for Board Exams and Class Unit Tests, ask homework doubts in Q&A (with Accepted & Faculty Endorsed answers), and vote once in duplicate-proof school polls.

5. Realtime Chat, Replies, Study Groups & Privacy

7-Day Auto-Purge

Messages and attachments vanish automatically after 7 days.

Consent-First DMs

No phone numbers needed. Private DMs & study groups unlock only after acceptance.

Replies & Reactions

Quote-reply messages, react with emojis, and search channel history fast.

Lazy Downloads

Attachments & images stay unloaded until you tap Get or Preview.

Teacher Guide (Faculty Studio & Roles)

Guide for Subject Teachers, Class Teachers, and Admins using Teacher Studio

1. Subject Teacher vs Class Teacher Onboarding

Register directly under the Teacher tab — no staff code needed. Select your assigned Classes, Subjects, Designation/Qualification, and describe what you will teach or handle so the Admin can review and approve with 1 click.

2. Chapter Lessons & 20/Day Verified Notes

In Teacher Studio (/teacher/index.html), share unlisted YouTube class videos, Drive PDFs, and summaries. Teachers receive a 20 files/24h quota and an automatic Teacher Verified badge on uploaded notes.

3. 5-Min MCQ Quizzes & Homework Feed

Build quick auto-graded MCQ quizzes with explanations (answer keys stay hidden from students until submission) and track student scores. Post daily homework with due dates and reference links without grading overhead.

4. Class Teacher Approvals, Unit Tests & Privacy

Class Teachers can approve pending students and schedule Unit/Class Tests scoped strictly to their assigned class. All Teachers can post scoped class notices, endorse Q&A answers, pin/moderate taught class & #academics chats, and toggle Allow Student DMs anytime.

Admin & Lead Admin Unified Access

Both Lead Admin and Secondary Admins can seamlessly switch across Admin Core, Teacher Studio, and the Student Portal to publish lessons, quizzes, unit tests, and official school circulars. Lead Admin holds exclusive authority over role promotions, teacher protections, and full platform governance.

Quick FAQs & Security

How do I change my password or accent color?

Tap your name/avatar card at the bottom of the navigation rail (or in the mobile More menu) to update your password, profile info, and Material You color theme anytime.

How are passwords and privacy protected?

Passwords use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (20,000 iterations + unique salt). Sessions use 256-bit random tokens, and private 1-on-1 DMs & unjoined study groups remain strictly private to participants.
