What is VidyaSphere & Why Was It Built?

Created by students, for students

VidyaSphere is a dedicated, distraction-free digital ecosystem founded and engineered by a 14-year-old student, Sriramik Sanjiv Sahu (Class 9th Shraddha).

The Real Problem It Solves:

Whenever schools have holidays, exam prep weeks, or unexpected closures, students frequently scramble to find study notes. This usually forces students to give out their personal WhatsApp numbers, get added to messy, distracting groups filled with spam, and risk their privacy.

VidyaSphere eliminates that completely! It gives every student from Class 1 to 12 a unified platform where notes, schedules, homework discussions, and chats live in one clean place—without ever sharing your phone number.

2. Academic Notes Hub & Strict Storage Quotas

Class, Subject, and Chapter-wise organized repository

Students can browse and download verified study materials, handwritten summaries, and question banks. To protect mobile bandwidth and ensure free edge hosting for the school, strict upload limits are enforced:

Max 25 MB Per File

Direct PDF and image uploads are supported up to 25 MB per file. Files are automatically compressed using native Gzip streaming to shrink storage by 40-60%.

4 Uploads / 24 Hours

Each student can upload up to 4 direct PDF notes every 24 hours. Direct uploads stay active for 30 days before auto-archiving.

Permanent Google Drive Links: For large textbook scans or entire year syllabi, choose the "Google Drive Link" option when uploading. Drive links are permanent for the full academic year and do not count against your 15MB file limit!

4. Realtime Chat, Compressed Attachments & 7-Day Auto Purge

Consent-first messaging engineered for privacy and zero storage waste

VidyaSphere's messaging system is designed as a research-grade student communication protocol:

⏱️ 7-Day Ephemeral Auto-Purge

All messages (public channels, study groups, DMs, and file attachments) automatically vanish after 7 days. This ensures students leave zero permanent digital traces and keeps our edge database lightweight and within free tier limits.

Edit & Delete Anytime

Sent a typo or changed your mind? Students have full control to edit or delete their sent messages at any time. Edited messages display an un-intrusive (edited) indicator.

Scientific Lossless Compression

When students attach diagrams, homework photos, or PDFs (up to 15MB), files are compressed using native Web Standards Gzip streams. This slashes storage by 30-70% while guaranteeing 100% byte-for-byte exact original quality when decompressed.

Zero-Waste Lazy Downloads

To protect mobile users on strict 1GB/day mobile data plans, attachments are never automatically downloaded into the chat feed. Instead, a clean file card with size information is shown, and the file only downloads when explicitly clicked!

Consent-First Direct Messaging (DMs)

To prevent spam and protect privacy, students must send a "Request to Chat" before 1-on-1 messaging unlocks. The recipient must accept the invitation first. No one can send unsolicited messages, and you never have to share personal phone numbers.

Peer homework help and democratic voting

Academic Q&A Forum

7. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Common questions answered

What should I do if the site loads slowly on mobile data?

Indian mobile carriers (Jio/Airtel) sometimes filter edge hostnames. To fix this instantly, enable Secure DNS in Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Use secure DNS > Choose Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Google Public DNS. The site will load instantaneously!

Is my password and data safe?

Yes! All passwords are cryptographic hashed using PBKDF2 with 20,000 rounds and random salts. Plaintext passwords are never saved or sent to anyone. Sessions use 256-bit cryptographically random tokens stored in protected database tables.

Can other students see my personal phone number?

No! VidyaSphere never asks for your personal phone number. All peer communication happens directly within the platform's consent-first chat and discussion forums.
