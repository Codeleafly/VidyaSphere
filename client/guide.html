What is VidyaSphere & Why Was It Built?

VidyaSphere is a distraction-free school ecosystem founded and engineered by Sriramik Sanjiv Sahu (Class 9th Shraddha). Instead of sharing personal phone numbers in chaotic messaging groups just to get homework notes or exam datesheets, students get one verified academic space for Class 1 to 12.

1. Direct Registration & 1-Click Approval

Step 1

Create Account

Select your Class (1–12), division, and roll number, and set your username and password immediately.

Step 2

Live Waiting Screen

After signup, you are logged in to a real-time waiting screen while your profile awaits verification.

Step 3

Automatic Unlock

Once the Lead Admin approves with 1 click, your dashboard unlocks automatically—no activation codes needed!

2. Academic Notes Hub & Storage Quotas

Max 15 MB Per Direct File

Direct PDF and image uploads up to 15 MB are gzip-compressed automatically (saving 40–60% storage) and stay active for 30 days.

4 Direct Uploads / 24 Hours

Each student can upload up to 4 direct files per 24 hours. Google Drive links are unlimited and stay permanent for the full academic year!

3. Official Examination Timetables

Visit Exams & Timetable to filter schedules by your class, see live countdowns, hall/room numbers, syllabus topics, and Google Drive datesheet links.

4. Realtime Chat, Study Groups & 7-Day Auto-Purge

7-Day Ephemeral Auto-Purge

All chat messages and attachments automatically vanish after 7 days to keep storage light and privacy clean.

Consent-First DMs & Groups

1-on-1 DMs and Study Group invitations require recipient acceptance before messages unlock.

Lossless Compressed Attachments

Files up to 15MB are compressed losslessly with gzip and restore byte-for-byte on download.

Zero-Waste Lazy Downloads

Chat attachments never auto-download on mobile data; they download only when you tap Get.

5. Academic Q&A & Duplicate-Proof Polls

Ask homework questions in Q&A and mark the best response as the Accepted Solution. Vote in school polls backed by unique per-user constraints so every student gets exactly one vote.

6. FAQs & Mobile Install

How do I install VidyaSphere as an app?

Open VidyaSphere in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS), tap the menu/Share icon, and select Add to Home Screen or Install app.

What if the site loads slowly on mobile data?

Enable Secure DNS in Chrome (Settings > Privacy and Security > Use secure DNS > Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google Public DNS).

How is my password protected?

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (20,000 iterations + random salts). Plaintext passwords are never stored.
